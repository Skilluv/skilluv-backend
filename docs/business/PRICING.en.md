# Pricing

**Version 2 — target figures, to be validated by the market.**

None of these prices has yet been paid by anybody. They are written to be
argued with, not because they are settled. The only figure implemented and
frozen in the code is the mission commission (§2).

Three currencies, because three markets: **XOF** for West Africa, **EUR** for
Europe, **USD** for elsewhere. The amounts are not conversions of one another
— a European price converted into XOF is a price nobody in Cotonou will pay.

---

## 1. Talent

### Credit packs

Credits pay for search and introductions. Bought in advance, never expiring: a
credit that expires is a credit sold twice.

| Pack | Credits | XOF | EUR | USD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 50 | 60,000 | 90 | 100 |
| Growth | 200 | 200,000 | 300 | 330 |
| Pro | 600 | 500,000 | 760 | 830 |
| Enterprise | 2,000 | 1,500,000 | 2,300 | 2,500 |

The volume discount runs to about 25%. Beyond that the pack costs less to buy
than it costs to serve on the large accounts that actually use it.

### Pipeline subscription

Candidate tracking. Monthly, cancellable monthly.

| Tier | Recruiters | XOF/month | EUR/month | USD/month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 2 | 30,000 | 45 | 50 |
| Growth | 10 | 90,000 | 140 | 150 |
| Scale | unlimited | 250,000 | 380 | 420 |

### Recruitment as a service

A campaign run end to end. **Setup fee** of 300,000 XOF / 450 EUR / 500 USD,
then **8% of gross annual salary** on a confirmed hire.

The market rate is 15% to 25%. Ours is deliberately low: the platform has
already done half the qualifying work, and charging an agency's price for work
we did not do is a quick way to lose the second client.

**No fee if the hire does not happen.** No retainer.

### Recruiting contests

|---|---|---|---|
| Self-serve | The contest, registrations, the standings | 250,000 | 380 |
| Managed | Plus designing the tasks and pre-screening | 900,000 | 1,400 |

## 2. Work

### Bounties

**8% for the enterprise posting it.** The contributor receives the amount
announced — that is the point: a 50,000 XOF bounty is a 50,000 XOF bounty, and
the commission is visible on the payer's side.

To be revisited. 8% covers processing and dispute risk and no more; defensible
to get started and probably too low at scale.

### Missions

**15%, dropping to 10% after ten missions delivered.** Frozen at selection and
copied onto every invoice: what was charged in March stays readable in
November.

This is the only figure in this document already implemented.

### Studios

A team assembled by Skilluv for an engagement. **Target margin 20% to 30%**,
depending on how much coordination we carry.

Below 20% the coordination is unpaid and done badly. Above 30% we are a
consultancy with a database, and there are already plenty.

### Outsourcing

Work handed to Skilluv and distributed among contributors. **Target margin 15%
to 20%** — lower than Studios because the coordination is lighter.

## 5. Ecosystem

**Creator marketplace: 15%.** Low end of the market, chosen so that a creator
who sells little still earns something.

**Certifications: 50,000 XOF / 75 EUR** per track, paid by the enterprise when
it certifies its teams, never by the talent.

**Academy cohort: 3,500,000 XOF / 5,300 EUR** for twelve people over three
months.

**Corporate training subscription: 15,000 XOF / 23 EUR per person per month**,
from ten people.

---

## 6. Finance

The most sensitive pillar, because it is the only one where a talent's money
passes through.

**Advance on earnings: 3% of the amount advanced, capped at thirty days.** No
compound interest, no late penalty: repayment comes from the invoice, and if
the invoice never arrives Skilluv carries the risk.

**Insurance intermediation: standard introducer commission**, shown to the
person taking out the policy. An undisclosed introducer commission is a
conflict of interest.

**Training finance: income-linked repayment, capped at 1.4× the amount
financed, and nil below an income threshold.** The cap and the threshold are
what distinguish this from a consumer loan sold to somebody who cannot afford
it.

---

## 7. Consultation

**Advisory: 350,000 XOF / 530 EUR per day.** Two days minimum — below that,
understanding the context eats the engagement.

**Guided onboarding: included** in annual programmes, 600,000 XOF / 900 EUR
otherwise.

---

## 8. What this document is not

A promise. Every figure here is a hypothesis about what a market will pay, and
most will be wrong. The two that matter today are the mission commission
(implemented) and the bounty commission (implemented); the rest will be
revised on first contact with a real client.

What will not move is the rule governing all of them: **nothing in this
document is charged to a talent for access to an opportunity.**
